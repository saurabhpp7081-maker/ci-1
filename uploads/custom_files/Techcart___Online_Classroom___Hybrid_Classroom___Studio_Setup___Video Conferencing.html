TC12HUL AI provides a flexible and wide range of applications through a single IP cable, and transmits various video and audio signals with low latency to simplify the workflow. AI tracking is enable to subtly capture speakers based on their movements. With 12x optical zoom and 4K resolution, combined with AI tracking and smooth PTZ functions.

TC12HUL AI uses a new generation of Sony 1 / 1.8 inches, up to 8.42 megapixel high-quality UHD CMOS sensors to achieve 4K (3840×2160) ultra-high resolution images. It delivers 4K of brilliant colors and detailed video at 60fps, even in environments that are too dark or too bright.
